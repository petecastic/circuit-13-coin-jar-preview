House rules

  • Opt in: you open your own jar.
  • 1 drop a day, up to 10 coins in that drop. Resets 00:00 UTC.
  • Each jar holds at most 100. A drop that would go over is rejected in full.
  • Take out 1 coin, once a day. Only you can take coins out.
  • No admin key, no app fees. Wallet addresses only, no sign-ups.
  • Streak = UTC days in a row with a drop.
